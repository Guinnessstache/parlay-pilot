Legs

Read this before you bet. Every ticket here has a negative expected value — the sportsbook keeps a cut of every leg, and parlays multiply that cut. The "real chance" shown is the market's own estimate with the margin removed; it is not a prediction that beats the market. Injuries are pulled from ESPN and can lag late news. Same-game parlays are re-priced by the sportsbook, so the slip may show different odds. Prices move — always check the slip before you place it.
